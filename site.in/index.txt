<h1>Project status</h1>

<p>
Aim of this project is to provide a common project skeleton.
Should capture best practice in working with an LLM (asked of an LLM).
Also capture practice from personal experience.
With each project derived from this skeleton, aim to incorporate lessons learned.
</p>
<p>
The skeleton is aimed at projects as part of a larger <b>homelab</b> project.
There is a website associated with the homelab.
Projects pages are published to the common homelab website.
</p>
<p>
There are still some rough spots.
Have not <i>entirely</i> sorted records, episodes, phases, or tasks - but seems a good start.
</p>
<p>
The set of prompts inform and constrain the LLM well enough to produce result that I find acceptable.
</p>
