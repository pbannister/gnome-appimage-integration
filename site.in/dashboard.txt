<p>
The audit covers the whole desktop, not only this project's launchers: a launcher written by
another tool, a desktop entry that cannot be parsed, or an icon that no theme resolves is
reported too.  It is a point-in-time snapshot, taken by the build, and it is meant to be
re-run rather than trusted: <code>appimage-integrate audit</code>, and
<code>audit --check</code> to ask the update information as well.
</p>

<p>
This page is generated from the state file, so it cannot drift from the host: the version is
the one the built tools report, and the counts come from the tool reading the real desktop.
</p>
